<header> :::::: Arul Saba Bracelets :::::: <end>

This information is about level 100+ Arul Saba bracelets only.

<header2>Gem Types<end>

Bruised Brawler - Melee damage
Burning Plasma - Energy damage
Corroded Glory - Chemical damage
Eternal Juggernaut - Max health
Frozen Tundra - Cold damage
Infinite Moebius - Nano Resist and Max Nano
Jagged Landscape - Projectile damage
Rainbow-hued Sky - Radiation damage
Searing Desert - Fire damage
Silent Killer - Poison damage

<header2>Gem Slots, Locations and Requirements<end>

All previous gems are also required, so 5 Gem Bracelets needs Galactic, Stellar, Emporer, Monarch, and Arbiter Gems.

1 Gem (Arbiter / Scheol) - Level <highlight>100<end> - <highlight>288<end>ME / <highlight>306<end>EE
2 Gem (Monarch / Adonis) - Level <highlight>150<end> - <highlight>528<end>ME / <highlight>563<end>EE
3 Gem (Emporer / Penumbra) - Level <highlight>185<end> - <highlight>937<end>ME / <highlight>1035<end>EE
4 Gem (Stellar / Inferno) - Level <highlight>202<end> - <highlight>1665<end>ME / <highlight>1775<end>EE
5 Gem (Galactic / Alappaa) - Level <highlight>215<end> - <highlight>2100<end>ME / <highlight>2270<end>EE

Defrosting Galactic Gems from Alappaa requires 1800 Chemistry

<header2>Other Tradeskills<end>

There are EE and ME requirements for other steps in the process, but they would usually be lower than those for attaching the gems: 1000+ in each should be sufficient which is required for 3+ bracelets in any case.
