<header> :::::: Guide To Sector 10  :::::: <end>

<header2>How to get there: (Make sure you have inv space and backpacks)<end>

Head to the Unicorn outpost (ICC Transport, use the lift, head behind the Unicorns.  Once down the stairs use the same elevator for Sectors 13/28/35

<font color = green>Missions</font>

Talk to the Unicorn Duty Sergeant located to the east in the outpost. There are five missions to get from him.

<font color = blue>Kill 5 Alien Medics
Kill 5 Alien Communication Officers
Kill 5 Alien Leaders (Small Generals)
Collect 5 Alien DNA (Cage them)
Kill 20 Alien Immature Vectors</font>

You might as well get all the missions.

<header2>Sector 10 - Playfield<end>

<font color = red>-=-WARNING-=- </font>Regeneration Conducts, these snare you and heal mobs near you, so kill these first when you see them.
<font color = yellow>TIP </font>If you are melee and cannot hit them because you are too far away and snared, you might find you move quicker walking than running.

<font color = green>Loot</font>

The aliens will drop lots of loot (hence the bring backpacks), the common aliens drop:

Kyr'Ozch Weapons.
Alien Armor Materials (For AI Tank Armors)
Alien Activation Crystal (Tradeskill)
Solid Clump of Kyr'Ozch Bio-Material 
Clumps of Kyr'Ozch Bio-Material.
Alien Data Storage Crystal - Insight (Alien Augmentation Devices)
Inactive Nano Input Hood (Used for AI City General Hoods, Swatchs)

They also drop items, used for making pocket bosses spawning items.

Alpha Signal Relay
Rho Signal Relay
Sigma Signal Relay
Kappa Signal Relay

<font color = green>Items required for making Signal Relays</font>

4 x Broken and Powerless Signal Amplifier
4 x Kyr'ozch Fuel Gland
Inert Kyr'ozch Teleportation Relay

<font color = green>Items for Tools to type the Signal Relays</font>

X Compiler Unit (4 different types, one for each boss)
X Crystal - Left Fragment (4 different types, one for each boss)
X Crystal - Right Fragment (4 different types, one for each boss)

<header2>Tradeskilling a Relay<end>

Combine 4 Broken and Powerless Signal Amplifiers together. (625 ME) to get Powerless Kyr'ozch Signal Amplifier
Combine fuel gland x 4 with Telportation relay to end up with Powered Kyr'ozch Teleportation Relay (625 EE)
Powerless Kyr'ozch Signal Amplifier + Powered Kyr'ozch Teleportation Relay to get Untuned Kyr'ozch Signal Relay (687 EE & ME)
Combine an X crystal right fragment with a left fragment to get X Tuning Crystal (625 NP)
Then the X Tuning crystal with a X Compiler unit to get X Tuning Unit - Inactive (687 NP)

You can use an outside tradeskiller to do the above.

Right click the inactive tuning unit to active it (this makes it <font color = red>NODROP</font>.

Combine Untuned Kyr'Ozch Signal Relay with the Tuning unit (not used in the process, and no tradeskills needed) to get an X Signal Relay to pop one of these bosses.

Rho: East General
Sigma: North General
Kappa: West general
Alpha: Middle General

<header2>Alien Pocketbosses<end>

From the center area of the playfield, you can get to the boss spawn points.  You will reach a fence, and need to click a box on the ground to cross.  This should run a program in your NCU (Alien Bio-Transmission Encoder) to allow you to cross safely.

Do the same when leaving.

To spawn a boss, target the Teleport ring in the middle of the spawn area, and right click the finished signal relay.

The bosses are

<font color = green>Rho- Ilari Khazon Ra</font>   <font color = green>DIFFICULTY 3/10</font>

Easy, spawns adds, but you can probably kill him and ignore the adds.

<font color = green>Sigma - Chemist Khazon Ra</font>   <font color = yellow>DIFFICULTY 5/10</font>

Spawns flames that damage you, so stay out of the fire.

<font color = green>Kappa - Ankari Khazon Ra</font>   <font color = brown>DIFFICULTY 7/10</font>

Drains nano pool, fully, no heals, no mongo, nothing.  Just need to kill fast.

<font color = green>Alpha - Cha Khazon Ra</font>   <font color = red>DIFFICULTY 9/10</font>

Spawns tough adds, stop attacking, kill adds.
