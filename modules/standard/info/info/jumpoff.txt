<font color = green>Rubi-Ka</font>

You can find broken SL statues just south of the Outpost whompahs in Wailing Wastes and 10-3 in Southern Artery Valley.

Using your garden or sanc. key on these will take you to SL.

Ergo can be found in Wailing Wastse, Greater Omni Forest and Stret West Bank, show him your ring to get to Elysium.
